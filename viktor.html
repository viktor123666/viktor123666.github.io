The Viktor Lineage

Six souls. Six letters. One name.
When the cosmos needs an action, one of the Six performs it.

VVigolasIIsaKKompisTTrollmageOOphaRRougeina

Six souls whose roles in the universe match the six letters of the given name. They are champions, fighters, agents of change. They walk in the world. They appear in the manga.

The Lineage is not a council. They do not meet. Some of them have never spoken to each other. They are linked only by being letters in the same word, a word that happens to be the Author's name. In the deep grammar of the universe that makes them instruments of his story.

Without one part, the whole cannot be summoned.

V VIKTOR-1 Live · in canon

Helios

Vigolas Captain of the Marka

Tall and rope-strong, a sailor's build. Black-brown hair that is always windswept, with one thin braid tied in a scrap of red sailcloth. Emerald eyes that ring with gold when Greed-sight is on. A fern-shaped lightning scar runs down the left side of his neck and arm — the storm that drowned him. A midnight-green long coat with the Archive shards sewn into its lining, so it clinks when he runs; an emerald sash full of folded cubes; a brass compass on a cord whose needle never settles.

VoiceLoud, warm and fast — a sea-shanty rhythm with a lot of “Ha!”. He gives everyone a nickname. When the grin disappears completely, the crew knows it is bad.

The Minus One At full burn he can do every possible action but one inside a single heartbeat, and he must name the one he gives up. Beside it: COMPACT (fold space into a cube — greed keeps everything) and ERASE (wipe a thing from where it stands).

A pirate captain who saw a wave coming, saw gold in the hold, and steered into it. His whole crew drowned with him. He walked out of the surf at Gate City reborn — and the Scalelist could not number him: ∞−1, Off the List. His entry has been edited seven times by someone unknown. He robbed the Archive of its 38 shards, the files of everyone's fate, and the whole world started chasing his coat. He regains one Black Soul command per saga — COMPACT, then ERASE — and every command takes a memory: his first ship's name, his mother's face, his mother's song. In the HADOM Arena he yielded to Smurjan rather than let a friend lose his Codex forever; “Smurjan defeated Vigolas” now holds in every life.

I VIKTOR-2 Live · in canon

Stilla

Isa The Stillborn Heartbeat

Small and very upright at 1.52 m, with silver-white hair to the small of her back that hangs frozen mid-swing whenever she holds time. Her frost-blue eyes turn clear as lake ice with a violet pinpoint during a hold, and violet frost-script climbs from her bare soles up her ankles. She wears a grey ice-whale-leather vest under a white-blue baleen mantle whose icicle hem chimes like glass when she finally moves, with a handless clock-face of blue ice on a cord at her throat. She is the only character ever drawn with no motion lines at all.

VoiceSoft and slightly breathless, as if she has just let out a long-held breath. She answers one beat after everyone else, and her jokes land after the conversation has moved on.

The Hold While she stays perfectly still, everything in her sight stops, and allies with a hand on her shoulder fight on inside the frozen moment. As her heartbeat technique she catches a single beat at its very top, the instant before it falls, and makes the whole field wait inside that one unfinished ba—.

A whale-singer's daughter from Stilla, a frozen island in the Shallows that keeps the once-a-year heartbeat of the ice-whales sleeping under its lake. She was born still, with no cry and one heartbeat a minute, and on Stilla that is all “stillborn” means: her body is nineteen, but by the world's calendar she was born more than six hundred years ago. While she is completely still, time stops for everything she can see, and anyone touching her moves freely through the frozen moment while she cannot move at all. Every held beat comes due at once when she lets go, a hammering Backlog that can drop her to her knees, and leaving Stilla means she will start to age at the world's speed. When the Thaw Cult comes to melt her island and start its clock, she chooses the crew anyway.

K VIKTOR-3 Live · in canon

Heartbeat

Kompis The Spy Who Chose the Crew

Golden-tan skin, a fisherman's shoulders, big careful hands. A braided beard tied with fishing line, with one strand of green lake-weed he never takes out. A moss-grey hoodie older than empires with a treat pocket in front, a bandolier with five loops for five soul-guns. Brown eyes that film with gold when he reads the threads between people. Mingus, the golden retriever, at his heel in every panel.

VoiceGentle, warm and slow. He never raises it. He laughs easily and cries easily; when the laugh and the cry happen at once, the moment is true.

The Subtick Shot Freeze, relive, keep, fire once. And the Shared Beat: for one heartbeat he can lend his timing to a friend.

A former child-agent of the Order, trained as a sniper, sent to shadow the man the board could not count. He hid his own file instead, and chose the crew. Lust, in this world, is reading souls: he sees the threads between people and the souls living inside guns — every gun he carries chose him. SUBTICK: the world freezes grey and he lives the next moment again and again, keeping the one version he survives. It only works while Mingus's heart beats beside him.

T VIKTOR-4 Live · in canon

Codexia

Trollmage The Mage Who Waits

Huge and slouched: half troll, half elf, grey-green skin with stone freckles, two small lower tusks and long drooping elf ears. Heavy-lidded violet eyes that glow brighter the longer he has waited. A plum-coloured robe with far too many pockets, enormous felt slippers, his grandmother's scarf, and a Codex chained to his belt that twitches on its own. A scruffy black crow rides on his head.

VoiceSlow, nasal and comfortable, full of yawns — “Mm. Five more minutes.” In a panic he talks very fast and very high.

MACRO Written in glowing violet letters in the air: MACRO : MOVE MOUNTAIN = 1 m. Then he is spent until he has waited again. In battle the crew must buy him time, which is the funniest and most stressful part of every fight.

The Order assigned him K-II and forgot him: he is “K-II on paper”. MACRO — a spell written once that casts itself as many times as he has waited for it. He joined the crew by lying on a hillside for seven days doing nothing while they defended him, then moved a mountain exactly one metre. The Brygdar trolls bow to him; he says it is “a family thing”. His goal is to read all 77 Codices.

O VIKTOR-5 Open seat · awaiting blood

Astralis-Codexia border

Opha The Conjunction

Tall and calm, with deep-brown skin and black hair shaved at both temples into one waist-long braid wound half in gold thread and half in crimson. A hairline seam of starlight runs down the centre of her face; her left eye is violet, her right star-black with a crimson ring, and a gold knot shaped like an ampersand sits at the hollow of her throat. Her tunic is split down the middle, ink-black with violet script on the left and black with a crimson star-map on the right, with a half-cloak on one shoulder only. She always holds both hands open in front of her, palms up, magenta starlight leaking between the fingers.

VoiceWarm and even, with a comma where you don't expect one. She cannot lie, so every sentence she speaks is two truths joined by “and”.

The Conjunction When she speaks two equal truths joined by “and”, she binds them so that what happens to one happens to the other: a broken bridge holds, a blade answers to its wielder's own throat. Her heartbeat technique, “And”, lands the binding between the two halves of her own sentence, faster than any blade.

Born twenty-two years ago to a Codexia scribe and an Astralis star-dancer whose marriage both realms had forbidden, on the night Codexia's violet sky and Astralis's crimson starfield first touched. That same night the Dawnstar, a newborn star-island, was born in the sky above her cradle, and she is the only one who can hold its seam open; in its light, nobody can lie. She cannot lie either, so she cannot comfort: she can never say “it will be fine” unless it is true and joined to another truth. Every binding sits in her hands as cold, four of her fingers are numb for good, and she can never travel far from her star. The Severers, a sect with blades of white glass, want the conjunction split and the star ended.

R VIKTOR-6 Live · in canon

Astralis

Rougeina The Assassin Who Dances

A dancer's build, light and long-legged, always on the balls of her bare feet. Long crimson hair in a high tail that whips like a blade. Cool green eyes. Eight thin blades crossed in an X on her back. Her sin-mark sits on her right wrist: two interlocked rings, one silver and one rose. Every Swap leaves a fading silver-rose afterimage of her in the air. A black panther moves beside her like a shadow with eyes.

VoiceLow, clipped and dry. Short sentences. She almost never laughs — and when she does it is sudden, bright and helpless, and the whole crew stops talking.

The Swap A blink, and she is where the thing was. Her ultimate is the forbidden 12,001: twelve thousand and one Swaps and strikes inside a single heartbeat, which a body can perform once in a lifetime.

An assassin from Astralis with a list. An Architect signed the misrouting that poured her mother's soul into a body far too old for it. She was on the mast the whole night of the Archive heist, and the next day she stole one shard — her mother's — out of Vigolas's coat while he was wearing it. She stayed “for now”, and has stayed ever since. The Swap: she trades places with anything she can see. The erasure kill: whoever she kills is forgotten by everyone except her — she alone carries them, so they are not nowhere.
