The family name · the Nine Guardians

The Rönnkvist Council

Nine souls at the roots of the cork oak.
Without the Council, Ferdinand goes silent and the Seal fails.

RRunaÖÖgarNNivaNNjordKKvarVVidarIIskaSSmurjanTTova

Nine souls whose roles in the universe match the nine letters of the family name. They are guardians, watchers, keepers. They do not fight openly. They sit at the roots of Ferdinand the Cosmic Cork Oak and make sure his bark keeps regrowing: the bark the Architects cut for the Void-II Seal.

The Council meets once per cosmic year, on the night the moon stands directly under Ferdinand's lowest branch. Nine votes are cast, and the vote is always unanimous, because the Council never votes on outcomes. It votes on who speaks for the tree this year. It has been Smurjan three times. It has never been Kvar.

The Rönnkvist keep the tree alive.

Runa The First Rune-Speaker

Ancient. Pure-white braid that reaches the floor. Eyes the color of fresh tree-sap. Palms covered in burned-in rune-scars. Robe of cork-bark shavings and rowan leaves.

VoiceQuiet. The world goes quiet to listen back. When she speaks a rune aloud, listeners either learn it or forget how to hear.

Signature Speaks one of the 24 first runes. Reality reshapes around the meaning.

Trollmage's great-(many)-grandmother. She refuses K-rank because she predates the rank. The Eighth Codex is her work directly.

Inside the hollow of Ferdinand's lowest branch, sitting on a stool of raw rowan. Trollmage knelt without being told.

Thanatos

Ögar The Far-Eye Watcher

Tall, gaunt. Blindfold woven from cypress thread and golden retriever fur. Beneath the blindfold her eyes are silver coins. Removes blindfold only on the new moon, only for the dying.

VoiceSoft, certain. Speaks of futures as though they already happened — because to her they already have.

The only being Echo could not infiltrate. She wept silver coins at Mingus's burial-that-never-happened. The coins are still in the Spiral.

At the rim of the Spiral Graveyard the night Mingus first didn't die. She arrived to mourn and stayed to celebrate.

Niva The Untraversed

Short, weathered, dark Sami complexion. Eyes like silver coins (no iris, no pupil — just reflective silver). Bone-white parka stitched with reindeer sinew and bone toggles. Single carved wooden stake.

VoiceSparse. Three words where someone else would use thirty. Nothing wasted.

Bakasura tried to consume her three times. Each time she gave him a piece of her parka instead. A piece of her parka was on Mingus's collar in S4 — that's how he survived the Lake.

Njord The Tide-Reader

Weathered Nordic fisherman, sixty-something. Salt-bleached blonde-grey beard to his chest. Sea-blue cloak worn to grey. Wooden tide-rod with seven knots. Hands bigger than his face.

VoiceSlow. Ocean cadence. Pauses between sentences long enough to fish in.

Signature Reads the lake. The lake reads back. He knows when Vigolas is angry, when Kompis is hurt, when Mingus has crossed.

Kompis's distant cousin. They drink mjölksta together every cosmic year on the dawn after Vigolas's birthday. He forecast the Stormcoast War seven months out and told no one — because being told doesn't change a tide.

LUMEN

Iska The Spark-Carrier

Child-aged but ancient-eyed. Around seven in appearance. Copper-red hair in twin long braids. Heavily freckled. Pale skin. Warm hazel eyes that look older than her face. Carries a small clay lamp glowing pure white flame.

VoiceBright. Direct. Says exactly what she means with the cheer of a kid and the certainty of a mountain.

Signature Holds up the lamp. The viewer remembers their happiest moment. The viewer chooses to fight harder, or to stop fighting.

Lit the lamp at Mingus's birth — which is why his coat glows in low light. Will light the lamp at the Last Dawn. Bakasura laughed exactly once, the time he saw her flame.

At the central altar of Lumen on the night the realm went dark. She walked in carrying the lamp. Lumen has not been dark since.

S RÖNNKVIST-8 The bridge · both orders

Trinity

Smurjan The Third-Voice

Three faces folded into one body on most days — Bali (black braid), Kali (red braid), Fused (white braid). Triadic robes patterned in Trinity's blue-violet + red-fire + white-dawn. Walks in the three-step gait: left, right, center, repeat. Since S4: a fourth, faint, silver-violet flicker at the edge of his peripheral vision — Echo, sealed.

VoiceThree voices in conversation with each other, audible to attentive listeners. Bali speaks in question form. Kali in declaration. Fused brings consensus and the third syllable. Since S4: Echo's voice occasionally surfaces as a fourth murmur, instantly suppressed by the Triad. Smurjan never lets her speak.

Three-Step Vote Bali speaks no, Kali speaks yes, the Fused tiebreaks. The decision binds the next cosmic century. Most decisions are not put to the Three-Step. The ones that are reshape realms.

The Third-Voice is the bridge soul: named in Rönnkvist (S of Rönnkvist) AND a Lineage champion. The only soul whose letter-position is in the family name. Without Smurjan, the Council cannot speak — he is the rowan-branch sound in their voting. In S3 he refused Echo's offer to join the Triad. In S4 his body became the seal that holds Echo permanently inside as the Tetrad. His Triad voted in 3/9 cosmic years; the abstaining 6 are recorded as failures of will, not consent.

Origins OVA Episode 5 (The Void Trinity). Defining arc in Season 3's Trinity War and Season 4's Echo's Last Stand.

Tova The Thunder-Herald

Tall, athletic warrior. Raven-black hair in a single long braid down her back. Copper armor with engraved bell-plates that catch the light. Brass herald's horn slung across her chest on a leather strap. Dawn-gold colored eyes. Strong jaw. Confident.

VoiceResonant. Carries. When she speaks at full volume, nine realms hear her. The tenth (NULL) hears nothing — which is its own message.

Signature Sounds the horn. The news lands true in twelve realms simultaneously. No translation needed.

Heralded the Eighth Codex's binding. Will herald the Ninth. Announced Mingus's death across all realms — and one heartbeat later announced his return.

At the Twelve Gates of Scalelist the dawn after Vigolas became Stormhold's keeper. She blew the horn. Every realm heard it. Every realm understood.
