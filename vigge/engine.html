The rules it will not break

What the engine refuses to do

These are commitments, not settings. They hold on every job.

It will not colour a line it is unsure of

When two people talk over each other and the match is not certain, the caption stays uncoloured. Fewer coloured lines beats putting your name on someone else's sentence.

It will not fill a quota

There is no target number of clips. A quiet recording returns few, or none, with the reasons attached. A brilliant one returns everything that cleared the bar.

It will not ship an unverified caption

The finished file is sampled and the pixels are checked. A caption that failed to render is caught before delivery, not by you afterwards.

It will not charge for a failure

A job that produces nothing costs nothing. There is exactly one place in the whole system where a customer is charged, and it runs after the files exist.

It will not put a slur in your feed

Masked in text, bleeped in audio — and moments that read as racist are removed from selection entirely, because a bleep does not save a clip that is about the joke.

It will not train on your content

Your source is deleted once you collect the results. Nothing you upload teaches the engine anything.
