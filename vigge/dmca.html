Copyright & takedowns

If material processed through ViggeClips infringes your rights, tell us and we will act quickly.

ViggeClips is a tool people point at their own recordings, and every user confirms they hold the rights before anything is processed. If that promise was broken and your work was used without permission, here is how to have it removed.

What to send

Email copyright@vigge.pro with:

  • Identification of the work you hold the rights to.
  • The specific ViggeClips URL or clip involved.
  • Your contact details.
  • A statement that you believe the use is unauthorised, and that the information you provide is accurate.

What happens next

We aim to review takedown notices within 3 business days. Valid notices result in the material being removed and, where warranted, the account being suspended. We keep a record of notices to handle repeat infringers.

Counter-notice

If your material was removed and you believe that was a mistake, reply to our email with your reasons and we will re-review.
