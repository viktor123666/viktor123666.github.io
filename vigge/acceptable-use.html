Acceptable use policy

The engine already refuses certain material automatically. This page says the same thing in plain words so there are no surprises.

The short version
  • Upload only footage you own or have permission to use.
  • No content that is illegal where you or we operate.
  • The engine auto-rejects extreme material — clips are never built from it.

1 · You must have the rights

Every upload requires you to confirm you own the recording or have the rights holder's permission. Uploading someone else's stream without permission is not allowed and is your responsibility, not ours.

2 · What we will not process

ViggeClips will not knowingly clip material that depicts or promotes: child sexual abuse, terrorism or violent extremism, real non-consensual sexual content, or content whose purpose is to harass or incite violence against a person or group. The engine's content gate refuses such moments automatically — no clip is ever produced from them.

3 · Enforcement

We may suspend an account that repeatedly uploads material breaking this policy, and we will cooperate with lawful requests. A suspended account keeps access to already-delivered clips for the normal 30-day window unless the law requires otherwise.

4 · Reporting

To report content or a copyright concern, see our takedown page.
