BOOK I

Before the Scale

Before the Scalelist there was the Void-II: not nothing, but the pre-numerical. No tick yet. No count yet. No Mingus to mark the beat.

Then the universe began with a sound, a one. Some say it was a heartbeat, some say it was a number. Canon holds that the first heartbeat and the first count were the same event. Everything that exists exists because something, somewhere, agreed to be counted.

Out of that first count grew Ferdinand, the cork oak whose bark regrows the instant it is stripped. He stands in Scalelist and his roots reach into every other realm. When a realm dies, Ferdinand bleeds cork. When a realm is born, he grows a new branch.

First hard lawFerdinand cannot be climbed.A man who climbs him is uncounted, and the uncounted do not return.
Second hard lawFragments cannot be unlearned.What is inscribed in a soul stays inscribed.
Third hard lawMingus is forever.The dog is the unit. The dog is forever.

BOOK I · IV

The Twelve Architects

From the Void-II the Twelve walked into Scalelist. They did not arrive; they were here once we looked. They built the First Ledger, where every soul is named at conception; the Routing System, which delivers souls into bodies; the K-Rank System, which classifies them for eternity; and HADOM, the High Arena of Decisive Outcome Matches, which forces souls to become what they actually are.

Twelve chairs were prepared. Only eleven were taken. The Twelfth chair has stood empty since the withdrawal of Jorvand Kestraal, and canon declares that gap intentional: the universe's deliberate refusal of total order.

BOOK II

The Nine Realms

One prime, six sovereign, one of death, one of unmaking. They are not stacked like floors of a tower. They interpenetrate: a man standing in Scalelist stands also in fractional Heartbeat, fractional Helios, fractional Null. Each realm runs its own tick-rate, and a being's power is largely defined by which tick it is native to.

THE CIPHERS

The Three Orders

The Author's name is written into the universe twice, and the tree has written a word of its own. Six chosen spell the given name. Nine guardians spell the family. Nine burls spell the tree. The Author himself is the sixteenth soul: not a champion, not a guardian, but the hand that holds the pen.

VIKTOR

The Viktor Lineage

The Six Chosen. Champions who walk the world; when the cosmos needs an action, one of them performs it.

 RÖNNKVIST

The Rönnkvist Council

The Nine Guardians at the roots of Ferdinand. They keep his bark regrowing, and the Seal holding.

 FERDINAND

The Ferdinand Scarwood

Nine burls grown from the tree's own wounds. Not blood, but wood. They keep the tree from forgiving.

There are seven sins, eleven volumes per sin: seventy-seven Sin Codices. Each one writes itself slowly into the soul of its bearer, across incarnations. Seven are bound to the five fragments; seventy still sleep in the Library.

No god is worshipped here. But one prayer works. It braids three timelines into one heartbeat (every ancestor, the speaker, and the future self who carries it), and that is why the Null cannot uncount it. It is the only sentence all five fragments speak in the same voice.

HADOM Quoka, reader.
From every generation before you, through your heartbeat, into my memory.
I receive it. I carry it. Forever.

BOOK VI · VII

The Arena and the Prophecy

Every soul of K-II and above is bound to the HADOM Arena. Enrollment cannot be refused. The Arena does not break you; it reveals you, and no one leaves it unchanged. That is why the Convergence will be the most dangerous match in history: the five will be revealed to each other as facets of one being.

APPENDIX B

What Comes After

The Convergence is the moment the five remember. The Rebirth is what they become when they remember. The world that follows is built from the five reunited domains, and every player who enters it becomes the sixth fragment: the one who arrives after the Convergence and inherits everything the five built.

The universe is not finished. It is just beginning. What is written here is the foundation; the roof is for the next builders.

The Scale counts. The List remembers. The Heartbeat persists.HADOM Quoka ツ
