Dolk

The Knife-Child

“Dolk — The Knife-Child.”

RealmThe Cut — the wound in Ferdinand's bark that keeps the Void-II SealHADOMTempo 0, he acts between beats · heartbeat technique: The Skipped BeatFragment—LineageFERDINAND-4StatusFerdinand's Scarwood · grown, not born

Look

A thin child-shaped thing, 1.25 m of ribs and elbows, with grey bark-skin smooth as peeled wood, huge dark eyes rimmed in indigo, and black hair he hacks short himself. From the elbow down his right forearm is a long blade of black obsidian with an indigo edge, which he holds carefully away from his body and from everyone. He is wrapped like a mummy in grey-brown scab-strips, each one a bond, a contract or a name he has cut. Only the wound is left bare: a long cut across his chest, shoulder to hip, that never closes, never bleeds, and glows faintly indigo inside.

Voice

Small and flat. He apologises before every cut, and cuts anyway.

Canon Connection

The fourth of the Ferdinand Scarwood, and the saddest. When the Architects cut Ferdinand's bark to make the Void-II Seal, he was the cut; the wound hardened into a burl, and the burl into a child. The wound across his chest is that first cut, still open, and as long as it stays open the Seal holds, so he stands guard at the Cut alone, seen only from outside. He must carry everything he severs, and he cannot hold a hand, a cup or a gift without cutting it. There are five things he cannot cut, the same five the Null cannot uncount: a heartbeat, the HADOM Quoka, love, Mingus, and the Count.

Signature Move

The First Cut — one silent stroke severs what has no body: an alliance, a contract, a rank, a name off a face. His heartbeat technique, The Skipped Beat, cuts the gap after one of your heartbeats so that the next one never comes, and you stagger a beat late; he acts between beats, never in them.

First Appearance

Saga VI · The Third Sign, at the Cut — the Saga Edition manga (in production).
