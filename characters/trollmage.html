Trollmage

The Reader · Master of the Eighth Codex

“Reads the Seven Sin Codices backward. Bound the Eighth. The Apprenticeship line is his.”

RealmCodexia — The Seven SpiresHADOMMacrofiable — variable by command, no fixed tickK-RankK-VFragmentSloth (absorbed) + Gluttony (incomplete)LineageVIKTOR-4StatusLive · in canon

Look

Violet-runed face from his backward-reading discipline. Deep purple eyes that have seen too many pages. Scholarly robes with rune embroidery at the hem. Holds a glowing codex page in the air with one hand more often than not. The other hand always still — too much movement breaks a backward-read.

Voice

Measured. Quiet. The cadence of someone who has learned to think in reverse without telling you. When he does speak, the sentence finishes its meaning before the words finish arriving.

Canon Connection

Master of the Seven Sin Codices, all read backward and bound. Bound the Eighth Codex (the Apprenticeship binding) at the climax of S3 — sealed Vex (the Forward Reader) and Pride (the possessed hand) inside. Runa is his great-(many)-grandmother and the original carver of the runes he reads. Apprentice Nock is his current disciple; the Eighth Codex's Apprenticeship binding ties them together.

Signature Move

Backward Read — speaks the last word of a Codex page first, then the second-to-last, then the third-to-last, until the page is complete. The page's *meaning* assembles itself in reality as he reads. Most listeners go briefly mad. He has not.

First Appearance

Origins OVA. Major arc in Season 2 (*Macro Wars*). Definitive arc in Season 3's *Forward Reader* and Season 4's *Nock's Right Hand*.
