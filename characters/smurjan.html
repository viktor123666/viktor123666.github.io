Smurjan

One Body, Three Souls · The Trinity

“Smurjan, Kali and Bakasura. Three shadows, one third eye, and the one exact point.”

Look

Tall, gaunt and very still, slate-grey skin, a long white braid, and three eyes — the third, in the centre of his forehead, rimmed with crimson when it opens. Faint crimson script-scars cover his chest and arms like writing under the skin. A torn tri-coloured robe in void-black, hunger-red and ash-grey. Barefoot. Wherever he stands, the light throws three shadows that do not match him.

Voice

Quiet and measured; he calls the body “we”. When the others cut in, their voices come out of his mouth — Kali sharp and fast, Bakasura slow and deep — and under it all murmur the voices of everyone the Trinity has carried.

Canon Connection

One body, three souls: Smurjan (Wrath, the host), Kali (who borrows another fighter's sin for sixty seconds) and Bakasura (a colossal maw that eats what it bites). Whatever dies in Trinity comes back slightly altered, and so does he — every respawn changes him a little. He met the crew as an enemy and joined them on his knees. In the HADOM Arena he defeated Vigolas, and the captain's lightning opened his Wrath Codex. He wants to free the voices inside him, and to find out who split him.

Signature Move

The Sightline — a crimson line drawn from his third eye to the one exact point of a thing, which he walks along step by perfect step and strikes with an open palm.

First Appearance

Saga I · The Satchel, chapter 9, Three Shadows — the Saga Edition manga (in production).
