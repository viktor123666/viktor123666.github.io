Isa

The Stillborn Heartbeat

“Isa — The Stillborn Heartbeat.”

RealmStilla — a frozen island in the ShallowsHADOMTempo 1 · heartbeat technique: The HoldFragment—LineageVIKTOR-2StatusLive · in canon

Look

Small and very upright at 1.52 m, with silver-white hair to the small of her back that hangs frozen mid-swing whenever she holds time. Her frost-blue eyes turn clear as lake ice with a violet pinpoint during a hold, and violet frost-script climbs from her bare soles up her ankles. She wears a grey ice-whale-leather vest under a white-blue baleen mantle whose icicle hem chimes like glass when she finally moves, with a handless clock-face of blue ice on a cord at her throat. She is the only character ever drawn with no motion lines at all.

Voice

Soft and slightly breathless, as if she has just let out a long-held breath. She answers one beat after everyone else, and her jokes land after the conversation has moved on.

Canon Connection

A whale-singer's daughter from Stilla, a frozen island in the Shallows that keeps the once-a-year heartbeat of the ice-whales sleeping under its lake. She was born still, with no cry and one heartbeat a minute, and on Stilla that is all “stillborn” means: her body is nineteen, but by the world's calendar she was born more than six hundred years ago. While she is completely still, time stops for everything she can see, and anyone touching her moves freely through the frozen moment while she cannot move at all. Every held beat comes due at once when she lets go, a hammering Backlog that can drop her to her knees, and leaving Stilla means she will start to age at the world's speed. When the Thaw Cult comes to melt her island and start its clock, she chooses the crew anyway.

Signature Move

The Hold — while she stays perfectly still, everything in her sight stops, and allies with a hand on her shoulder fight on inside the frozen moment. As her heartbeat technique she catches a single beat at its very top, the instant before it falls, and makes the whole field wait inside that one unfinished ba—.

First Appearance

Saga IV · The Twelfth Chair, Arc 3: Stilla — the Saga Edition manga (in production).
