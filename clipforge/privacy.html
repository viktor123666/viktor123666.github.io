Privacy notice

Written to be read. If anything here is unclear, ask and we will fix the wording rather than explain it away.

The short version
  • Your uploaded footage is deleted once you have collected your clips.
  • We never train models on your content.
  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it at any time.

Who is responsible

ClipForge is operated by Scalelist Universe, Sweden. For any question about your data, or to exercise the rights described below, contact privacy@scalelistuniverse.com.

We are the data controller for your account information, and a data processor for the video material you upload.

What we collect and why

Account data

Your email address, so we can create the account and send sign-in links and job notifications. Legal basis: performance of a contract.

Uploaded video and audio

The stream you submit, for the sole purpose of producing your clips. Legal basis: performance of a contract. This is the only reason it exists on our systems.

Derived data

Transcripts, detector measurements and, if you choose to create them, voice profiles. A voice profile is a mathematical summary of vocal characteristics — it cannot be played back or reconstructed into audio.

Billing data

Handled by Stripe. We store a customer identifier and your subscription status. We never see or store your card number.

Technical logs

Job timings, error messages and aggregate usage, so we can keep the service working and bill correctly. Legal basis: legitimate interest.

What we do not do

  • We do not train models on your content. Your footage improves your clips and nothing else.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not publish anything on your behalf without an explicit action from you.
  • We do not read your footage for any purpose other than processing the job you submitted.

How long we keep things

Data Retention
Uploaded source video Deleted when you collect your results, or 7 days after the job finishes
Finished clips 30 days, then deleted
Transcripts and measurements 30 days, with the clips
Voice profiles Until you delete them or close your account
Account and invoices While your account is open; invoices kept 7 years as Swedish accounting law requires

Voice profiles — extra care

A voice profile is biometric data under the GDPR, so it gets stricter handling. We only create one when you explicitly ask us to, by uploading a recording for that purpose. It is used solely to colour subtitles and weight clip selection within your own account. It is never shared, never used to identify anyone outside your account, and deleting it is immediate and permanent.

If you upload a recording of someone else's voice, you are responsible for having their consent.

Who processes data for us

  • Object storage and CDN — stores uploads and results, EU region.
  • Compute providers — our own servers plus on-demand GPU capacity for transcription.
  • Stripe — payments and invoicing.
  • Email delivery — sign-in links and job notifications.

Each is bound by a data processing agreement. Where a provider operates outside the EEA, transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, portability, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to privacy@scalelistuniverse.com and we will respond within 30 days.

You can delete your account yourself from the dashboard, which removes your footage, clips and voice profiles immediately. Invoices are retained as law requires.

If you believe we have handled your data badly, you may complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Cookies

We use a single essential cookie to keep you signed in. No advertising or tracking cookies, and therefore no consent banner — because there is nothing to consent to.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to production systems is limited to those who need it. Download links are signed and expire.

We will tell affected users without undue delay if a breach puts their data at risk.

Changes

If we change this notice materially we will email you before it takes effect. The date at the top always reflects the current version.

Status
