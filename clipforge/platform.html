01API & MCP

The engine is built to be driven by more than a human with a mouse. Two ways in, and they are different things.

REST API

Everything the workspace does happens over the same HTTP calls you can make yourself: request a sign-in link, start an upload, queue the job, follow its stages, and collect the clips.

EndpointDoes
POST /api/auth/requestSends a one-time link. Answers identically whether or not the address has an account — the page must not reveal who exists.
GET /api/auth/callbackRedeems the link and sets the session cookie. Single use, 15 minutes.
GET /api/auth/meWho you are and how many minutes you have left.
POST /api/upload/beginSplits the file and returns one URL per part.
POST /api/upload/completeJoins the parts and makes the source runnable.
POST /api/jobsQueues the source and places the credit hold.
GET /api/jobs/:idStage, percent, the clips — and every skipped moment.
POST /api/clips/:id/report"This one was not good enough" — one sentence, straight into our improvement queue.
POST /api/links/acceptTwitch/YouTube/Kick VOD by link. Refuses without rightsConfirmed.

Status: the API runs and is verified end to end — sign-in, multipart upload, queue, credit holds — but on localhost only. There is no public address to call until it is deployed.

MCP — a local tool

The MCP server lets an AI assistant on your own machine read the balance, list jobs, submit one, and collect clips. Your AI client's configuration starts it as a local process — it speaks over standard in/out, not the network.

What it is NOT
  • There is no network endpoint to connect to through this page.
  • It runs on your machine, against your account, fixed at startup — no userId travels with a call, so there is no way to ask for anyone else's data.
  • Read tools cannot spend credits. Anything that costs money requires an explicit call.

03Rights to the material

We ask, and we keep the answer.

Before a link is fetched or a file is queued you confirm the material is yours, or that you have the rights holder's permission. Without that confirmation the system refuses — POST /api/links/accept answers with an error and nothing is fetched. The confirmation is stored with a timestamp.

"I confirm this recording is my own content, or that I have the rights holder's permission to process it."

Deliberately a confirmation, not a disclaimer. A service that says "we do not look and we know nothing" has nothing to show the day someone asks. A stored confirmation does. The protection is the evidence, not the looking away.

What we do not do: we do not editorially review content and cannot judge whether a confirmation is true. Responsibility for the material stays with whoever uploads it — but the question was asked, and the answer is kept.

04Security and pre-launch status

The strongest control is architectural: your source video is deleted once you collect your clips, at the latest after seven days. Clips last thirty. A breach can only expose data that still exists.

AreaToday
CertificationsNone — no SOC 2, no ISO 27001, no external penetration test.
FirewallHost-level intrusion blocking. No bug bounty programme.
Sign-inSingle factor. Passwordless means your account security equals your email security. No 2FA yet.
OperationsOne region, one render cluster. No SLA. Outside working hours: best effort.
DeletionProven, not claimed — the sweep ran on real data and removed 3.74 GB of source while the clips stayed.
PaymentsStripe is built but switched off. No card details touch the system.
Before strangers may register
  • A data protection impact assessment (DPIA) for voice profiles — planned, not started.
  • Swedish legal review of terms, privacy notice and DPA — planned, not started. The brief with thirteen questions is written and waiting.
  • Company name, registration number and complaints address in the footer.

None of these block freelance work. A freelancer processes material for a client under a direct agreement. The review is required the day unknown people register themselves.

05Fair expectations — read before paying

Where we are not the right choice
  • The hype score is a measured heuristic, not a prediction of views. We will not pretend otherwise.
  • No template library, no brand kit, no team seats, no mobile app.
  • Kill counts are read from what people say, not the game HUD. A silent triple kill is invisible.
  • You cannot enrol your own voice yet. Speaker separation works, but teaching the engine which voice is yours is done by hand, by us.
  • We are new. No case studies beyond our own channels. If you need a vendor with a track record, that is not us yet.

Turnaround, measured: a 115-minute stream took 65 minutes from queue to finished clips on a consumer RTX 3060 Ti — 0.57× the stream's length, six clips, fifty skipped moments logged. A measurement on a real file, not an estimate.

Try it yourself

cd F:\scalelistuniverse\platform
npx tsx src/ops/solo.ts app

  → open http://127.0.0.1:8787/clipforge/login.html
  → type any email address
  → the link prints in the window AND to clipforge-local\SIGN-IN-LINK.txt
  → paste it, upload a file, start the render engine in a second window:

npx tsx src/ops/solo.ts report     the delivery note for the client
npx tsx src/ops/solo.ts sweep      deletes delivered clients' source files
npx tsx src/ops/solo.ts feedback   every clip report customers have filed

Listens on localhost only. Nothing is reachable from outside, not even from your own wifi.
